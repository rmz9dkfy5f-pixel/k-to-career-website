# K to Career Website Action Plan

## Clarify the Core Objective
Decide whether the website’s primary purpose is to attract students and parents, secure funding from donors and grants, or recruit industry partners and volunteers. One clear objective should control the site strategy.

## Segment the Audience
Separate the main audience groups:
- Students and parents
- Industry partners
- Donors and volunteers

Each group should have its own message, proof, and call to action.

## Gather Evidence of Impact
Collect proof that builds trust:
- Program outcomes
- Success stories
- Partner testimonials
- Certifications earned
- Participation numbers
- Placement, internship, or scholarship results

## Draft the Site Map
Build the site around these pages:
- **Home** — mission, value proposition, primary call to action
- **Programs** — offerings, schedules, costs, eligibility, registration
- **Impact** — metrics, stories, outcomes, testimonials
- **Partners** — current partners, collaboration benefits, inquiry path
- **Get Involved** — donations, volunteering, sponsorships
- **About Us** — leadership, governance, operations, credibility
- **News and Events** — workshops, announcements, milestones
- **Contact** — simple contact paths for each audience

## Develop Targeted Content
Write direct, plain-language copy for each audience. Avoid broad nonprofit language that sounds good but says little. Use visuals, examples, and specific outcomes to support claims.

## Plan Technical Requirements
Set the baseline requirements:
- Mobile-first design
- Accessible structure and readable contrast
- Content management system
- Analytics and conversion tracking
- Fast load times
- Clear forms and contact flows

## Launch a Pilot and Iterate
Release a first version, test it with real users from each audience segment, gather feedback, and tighten weak pages before wider promotion.

## Immediate Next Moves
1. Choose the primary website goal.
2. Pick the top audience to optimize for first.
3. Collect proof points and operating details.
4. Approve the site map.
5. Start homepage and programs page copy.
