Our Purpose

A nonprofit STEM ecosystem built for the full career journey

K to Career connects learners from early childhood through post-secondary and early career entry to real-world STEM opportunities, industry professionals, and career pathways — with a focus on underrepresented communities.

Mission

Empower learners of all ages to thrive in STEM fields through industry-connected educational experiences that bridge classroom learning and career success. Through strategic partnerships and personalized pathways, we inspire curiosity, develop critical skills, and create opportunities for diverse communities to participate in and lead the future of STEM.

Vision

A world where every individual has access to lifelong STEM learning that prepares them for meaningful careers, drives technological innovation, and builds economically vibrant communities — with curiosity nurtured from early childhood through professional development, and a diverse, skilled workforce ready for tomorrow's challenges.

What Students Gain

Program outcomes

PATHWAYS is designed to build five specific competencies. These reflect what students work toward in each session — not projected results, but what the program is built to develop.

01
STEM Content Mastery
Students apply rigorous STEM knowledge to investigate real-world questions and develop solutions across integrated science, technology, engineering, and mathematics domains.
02
Interdisciplinary Thinking
Students connect ideas across STEM fields and synthesize knowledge from multiple disciplines to approach complex, multi-faceted problems.
03
Inquiry & Critical Thinking
Students conduct research, refine questions, and apply logical reasoning using scientific, engineering, and mathematical practices to investigate real challenges.
04
Collaborative Leadership
Students work in multidisciplinary teams, develop subject-matter expertise, and build the ability to communicate and lead across diverse groups.
05
Professional Readiness
Students leave with practical skills, career awareness, professional confidence, and a network of peers and mentors in STEM fields.
06
Communication & Information Literacy
Students interpret and communicate STEM information using domain-specific vocabulary, develop evidence-based arguments, and collaborate with diverse audiences.
